about

You are alone in an early 1900s lighthouse. Daylight is for routine: fuel, cleaning, repairs, supplies. Night is for finding out whether routine was enough.

The fog thickens after sunset. Hunger and thirst make simple tasks feel heavier. The station begins to sound occupied.
